Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data GoGizbi! collects, why, and what rights you have. We collect as little as we can.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Konstantinos Xeras, trading as GoGizbi!, Kraków, Poland. Contact: hello@gogizbi.com. See our legal notice for full details.

2. What we collect and why

Contact form
Your name, email, topic and message, so we can reply to you (legal basis: steps you ask us to take, and our legitimate interest in answering questions — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and arrives in our email inbox.
Subscriptions and payments
When you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment. We receive your name, email address, billing country and which plan you bought — never your full card number. We use this to run your subscription and send your login (Art. 6(1)(b)), and keep invoices as tax law requires (Art. 6(1)(c)).
Your GoGizbi! account
Your login email and the videos and files you create, so the service works (Art. 6(1)(b)).
Website visits
We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly tool that uses no cookies and does not collect personal data or track you across sites. Our hosting provider (Render) keeps short-lived technical server logs, including IP addresses, for security (Art. 6(1)(f)).
Example videos
Video preview images load from YouTube (Google). Videos are only played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and only when you click play. YouTube may then process your data under Google's own privacy policy.

We do not use advertising cookies, and we don't sell your data.

3. Who we share data with

Only with the service providers we need to run GoGizbi!: Stripe (payments), Render (website hosting), FormSubmit (contact form), Google (email inbox and YouTube), ImprovMX (email forwarding), GoatCounter (visit counts), and the provider of the video-making software that powers the GoGizbi! app. Some of them are based outside the EU (mainly in the USA); in those cases transfers are protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

4. How long we keep it

  • Contact messages: up to 2 years after our last conversation.
  • Account data: while your subscription is active, and up to 90 days after it ends, then deleted.
  • Invoices and payment records: as long as Polish tax law requires (usually 5 years).

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw any consent you gave. Just email hello@gogizbi.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority — in Poland, that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl).

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and email subscribers about important changes.
