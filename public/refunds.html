Refunds & cancellation policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: cancel anytime in one click on your subscription page. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again. New subscribers can get a full refund within 14 days.

How to cancel

  1. Go to your subscription page (powered by Stripe).
  2. Enter the email address you used when you subscribed. Stripe will email you a secure login link.
  3. Click Cancel subscription.

You can also email hello@gogizbi.com and we'll cancel it for you. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period (month or year). You keep full access until then.

Your 14-day right of withdrawal

If you live in the EU, you have the right to withdraw from your subscription within 14 days of your first payment, without giving a reason. To do so, email hello@gogizbi.com with your name and the email address you used at checkout, and say that you want to withdraw. A short message is enough.

We will refund your first payment in full, to the same card or payment method, within 14 days of receiving your message, and close your subscription.

Renewals

Renewal payments (after your first billing period) are not refundable, because you can cancel at any time before a renewal. If you were charged by mistake or something went wrong, please contact us — we will always look at it fairly.

Changing plans

Want to switch between Starter, Pro and Agency, or between monthly and yearly? Email us and we'll take care of it.
